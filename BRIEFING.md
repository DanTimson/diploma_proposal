Eligibility here means that published/released results, inspectable artifacts and a comparison path exist. It does not mean that a new contribution is established. A dataset name appearing in two papers is insufficient: preprocessing, splits, candidate universe, labels, attack budget and metric denominator must also match.

**How to use the requested completion percentages.** Treat 5–10% as topic readiness: a specific question, a falsifiable contribution, baseline evidence, a defined experiment and initial code. Treat 20–40% as implementation readiness for the selected project: data ingestion, one baseline, configurations, evaluation, tests, run receipts and a launchable experiment. A harness with no experiments is not 40% of the scientific evidence, and should not be advertised to professors as such.

**Measured subgroup size.** The release contains 460 signed gold evidence pairs for 279 claims, with only 15 claims having both SUPPORT and CONTRADICT evidence. Overall signed evidence Recall@k, with precision/non-evidence burden reported alongside it, is the primary selection outcome. Both-stance coverage is a diagnostic, not a sufficiently powered primary endpoint. A broader contradiction-specific claim needs a second validated corpus; that is not yet an eligible benchmark in this starter.

**Beyond the diploma.** A is the stronger continuation of the original contextual-reputation idea; B is the easier route to a visible evidence-search demo. Both fit personal research and a diploma together. Neither currently justifies a full-time commercial commitment: there is no demonstrated new advantage, user demand or deployment-specific data advantage. Reconsider that only after a reproducible gain over simple baselines and a pilot with actual users.
