B: comparison contract and next implementation

**Reproduction anchor.** SciFact-Open Table 5, not the hidden SciFact-Orig test leaderboard. Pin `dwadden/scifact-open` at `6fc2e2f2f97001f2fadb882600beb3e984d77aad` and the data archive checksum in `evidence/sources.lock.json`. Released predictions plus labels are enough to replay pair-level P/R/F1 and AP. The official evaluator was run with only `astype(np.int)` changed to `astype(int)` for current NumPy. That change does not alter the arithmetic definition.

Keep the reported paper numbers distinct from local replays. Paper Table 5 averages 1,000 bootstrap resamples; a point estimate from the full release is not identical. MultiVerS in the paper corresponds to `multivers_20` in the release. Do not conflate that with `multivers_10`, which is a different training negative-sampling variant, not a top-10 retrieval setting.

**Two evaluation scopes.** Fixed-candidate analysis uses existing prediction lists and measures selection within those lists; report the candidate-set ceiling. Full-corpus retrieval searches 500,000 abstracts and requires separately running retrievers. `corpus_candidates.jsonl` is only roughly 12k pooled/citation candidates and must not be advertised as the full search corpus. The starter extractor deliberately omits the full corpus to keep the first audit small.

**Gold separation.** Construct ranking features from document text, model scores, predicted stance and observable provenance. Do not use annotated evidence presence, gold stance, `model_ranks` from annotation selection or the gold-derived conflicting-claim subset to select documents. Define subsets only after ranking. A gold-stance selection is allowed solely as a labeled oracle ceiling, never as a method.

**Leakage controls.** SciFact-Open reuses claims originating in SciFact. Do not train/tune on those same claims and call the resulting evaluation held out. For the first study use fixed pretrained outputs and either fixed parameters or source-document-grouped nested cross-validation for new selection parameters. Report original-model training provenance; no claim of completely unseen data is implied. New learnable retrieval methods need a documented overlap audit before fitting.

**Baseline ladder.** Score-only top-k; exact duplicate removal; MMR; simple predicted-stance quota; candidate soft-coverage method. Treat +VeriRel as necessary recent prior art and reproduce it if its code/configuration can be verified; it is not yet a ready adapter. Do not compare our new recall metric numerically against a published claim-accuracy metric.

**Controlled extension.** Add exact copies first, with distinct visible IDs and a hidden known copy-origin map used to evaluate duplication. An operational exact-hash deduplicator may use text hashes, while the oracle origin map remains unavailable to realistic methods. Paraphrase attacks are a later extension with a fixed generator and fidelity checks. Measure whether low-scoring counterevidence survives duplicate saturation. Natural source dependence is not annotated in this corpus; do not claim to solve it from these experiments.

**Metrics.** Pair-level signed P/R/F1 under the official convention; support and contradiction Recall@k; both-stance coverage on claims with both gold labels; non-evidence burden; selection latency. In incomplete-judgment analyses report judged coverage and treat unjudged as unknown, separately from the official protocol that treats unjudged as NEI. A new method that retrieves outside the pool needs additional assessment or a carefully qualified evaluation.

**Statistical scope.** The conflicting-evidence subgroup is small. Bootstrap by claim/source group, provide counts and intervals, and avoid declaring superiority from a few examples. Confirm on another corpus before broad generalization. This is a required strengthening step after the initial selection presentation, not an already completed benchmark.

**Before November 19.** Implement a saved-prediction adapter producing feature-only candidate lists; implement MMR and predicted-stance selection; add an evaluator for subgroup recall; validate with the already replayed official metrics; create 3-seed small perturbation checks and a run manifest. Test full extraction on one corpus record before allocating embedding/GPU work. A ready harness must execute at least one baseline and one perturbation end to end.

**Measured subgroup size.** The release contains 460 signed gold evidence pairs for 279 claims, with only 15 claims having both SUPPORT and CONTRADICT evidence. Overall signed evidence Recall@k, with precision/non-evidence burden reported alongside it, is the primary selection outcome. Both-stance coverage is a diagnostic, not a sufficiently powered primary endpoint. A broader contradiction-specific claim needs a second validated corpus; that is not yet an eligible benchmark in this starter.
